Living Towns 0.4.34 — Sleep routing recovery

- Expanded the named wall-sign scan to cover larger houses and signs mounted higher above the home door.
- If a villager cannot resolve its named sign after repeated scans, it uses a safe indoor/home rest position instead of standing still all night.
- If a doorway waypoint route is marked stuck or makes no progress, the villager releases the doorway reservation and retries directly to its assigned rest cell.
- The named sign remains the preferred sleep location; the safe home position is only a fallback.
- Keeps the 0.4.33 persistent bed assignment fix.
