LivingTowns 0.4.56
Target: Survivalcraft 2.4.0.0 / API 1.9.5.1

Personal outdoor work signs:
- Every villager uses a separate wall-mounted Wooden Sign.
- Put the villager's exact displayed name on one sign line and WORK on another line.
- The sign is searched for within 200 horizontal blocks of that villager's registered home.
- Search is incremental to avoid one large scan freezing the game.
- At dawn the villager leaves through its registered home door and travels to its own sign.
- At dusk the villager stops work and returns home.

Navigation:
- Long work-sign routes use a 30,000-position pathfinding budget.
- The existing six-block obstacle-detour search remains active.
- When the front approach is blocked, the villager tests alternate clear positions in front of its own sign.
- Unreachable detour waypoints continue to be temporarily blacklisted.

Existing seven villager templates, models, animations, language assets and XDB database are retained in the full .scmod package.
